How This Site Updates

Data Sources

Season statistics, assignments, schedules and game recaps are built from official MLB/MiLB data feeds. Historical MLB alumni and career totals are sourced from Baseball Reference.

The morning workflow publishes Mustangs Daily, assignments, transactions and the previous night’s recap. A second workflow refreshes current stats and today’s MLB games every 30 minutes.
