SLO LIFE

MORE THAN A PLACE TO PLAY BASEBALL.

Cal Poly sits in San Luis Obispo, where a true college-town downtown, nearby beaches and the hills of the Central Coast all become part of the student-athlete experience.

01 · Downtown

THE HEART OF SLO

Mission Plaza anchors downtown San Luis Obispo, with restaurants, coffee, shops, community events and the Thursday Farmers' Market all part of the city's college-town rhythm.

Explore Downtown SLO ↗
02 · Avila Beach

BEACH DAY, CLOSE TO HOME.

Avila Beach is about a 15-minute drive from San Luis Obispo and Cal Poly. The promenade, pier, paddleboarding, swimming and Bob Jones City-to-Sea Trail make it an easy Central Coast reset.

Explore Avila ↗
03 · Pismo Beach

SURF, SAND + THE PIER.

Pismo Beach is also roughly 15 minutes from downtown SLO, with surfing, beach volleyball, tidepools, the pier and the Pismo Preserve nearby.

Explore Pismo ↗
04 · Outdoor Life

TRAILS START NEAR TOWN.

Bishop Peak is one of SLO's signature climbs, while Cerro San Luis, Islay Hill, Reservoir Canyon and Poly Canyon give students plenty of ways to get outside without leaving the area.

GETTING HERE

THE CENTRAL COAST IS CONNECTED.

San Luis Obispo County Regional Airport (SBP) is served by Alaska, American and United, with nonstop routes that currently include Seattle, Portland, San Diego, Phoenix, Dallas/Fort Worth, Denver, Los Angeles and San Francisco, plus seasonal Las Vegas service.
